## Primary post

```
In the kasi, when someone borrows from a mashonisa, it's a handshake and a name in a notebook. Miss a payment and there's friction. But pay it back — faithfully, for years — and it counts for nothing. No bank ever sees it.

We just changed that in KasiKash.

When a mashonisa now records a loan, the borrower confirms it with their own ID — either on the spot, or by tapping a WhatsApp link on their own phone. Two things happen:

→ The loan is tied to a real person, so there's real accountability.
→ Every repayment starts building that person's credit record — a Financial Passport a formal lender can one day trust.

For the first time, paying back your neighbourhood loan can make you visible to real credit.

That's the whole idea behind KasiKash: take the trust the kasi economy already runs on — stokvels, mashonisa — and turn it into a financial identity that opens doors.

Built for real life: works offline, five languages (isiZulu, Sesotho, Afrikaans, English, Tshivenḓa), and free for the people who use it.

We're building the credit layer for the people and businesses the banks can't see.

👉 kasikash.com
```
