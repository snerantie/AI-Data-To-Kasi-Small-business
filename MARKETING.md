# KasiKash — Smart Marketing Playbook

A phased, SA-specific go-to-market plan for KasiKash. Built for the actual audience:
kasi small-business owners, stokvel treasurers, and the lenders who want to reach them.

**Companion docs:** [`LINKEDIN.md`](./LINKEDIN.md) · [`README.md`](./README.md)

---

## 🎯 The mental model

You have **two customers**, not one. Market them separately.

| Side | Who | What they care about | Where they are |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Traders** (users) | Spaza shops, salons, taxi ranks, phone-repair, stokvel treasurers | *"Will this help me get credit or run my hustle better?"* | WhatsApp, TikTok, Facebook, radio, taxi ranks |
| **Lenders** (buyers) | Bank SME divisions, microfinance, Yoco, Retail Capital, Merchant Capital | *"Can I trust this score, and how many users will it give me access to?"* | LinkedIn, press, industry events |

> **The wedge:** you don't need 100,000 traders before talking to lenders. You need **50 real, engaged traders + 1 signed lender pilot**. Everything below serves that.

---

## 📍 Phase 1 — The Beachhead (next 4 weeks)

**Goal: 50 real users in ONE community + 3 lender conversations.**

### 1a. Pick ONE community. Not "South Africa."

Choose based on where you personally have access:

- One **taxi rank** (Bree, Baragwanath, MTN Noord, Wanderers, etc.)
- One **township area** (a specific zone of Soweto, Katlehong, Khayelitsha, Mamelodi)
- One **stokvel network** (your family/church/neighbourhood group)
- One **hair salon strip** on a busy road

> 50 users clustered in one place = word-of-mouth ignition. 50 users scattered across SA = nothing. This is the single biggest early-founder mistake.

### 1b. On the ground

- **Print 200 A6 flyers** at Postnet (~R400 total). Front: logo + tagline. Back: 3-step "how it works" + QR code to `kasikash.com`.
- **Walk the rank/strip.** Talk to 20 traders. Show the app on YOUR phone. Ask 3 questions:
  1. *"How do you keep track of who owes you money?"*
  2. *"Have you ever tried to get a loan from a bank?"*
  3. *"If I told you this could help you get one, would you try it?"*
- **Install the PWA on 5 phones on the spot.** This is your founding cohort. Follow up on WhatsApp weekly.

### 1c. Lender outreach (in parallel)

Reach out to **3 lenders** for exploratory conversations (not sales):

1. **Yoco** — you already integrate. `partnerships@yoco.co.za`
2. **Retail Capital** or **Merchant Capital** — merchant cash-advance players hunting SME data
3. **Standard Bank BizConnect** or **Nedbank Small Business Services**

Pitch line:
> *"I have X users generating audited financial passports for informal SA SMEs. I'd love 15 minutes to show you the data model — no ask, just feedback."*

Even 1 LOI (Letter of Intent) becomes your credibility asset for the next 12 months.

## 🔄 Phase 2 — Growth Loops (months 2–3)

**Goal: 500 users, growing without your daily effort.**

Stop chasing individual users. Build **loops that reproduce themselves.** Three loops matter for KasiKash:

### Loop 1 — The Stokvel Loop 🟢 *(your unfair advantage)*

- Every stokvel treasurer using KasiKash brings **10–30 members** with them.
- The WhatsApp invite feature (PR #25) IS the loop.
- **Do this:** build a WhatsApp broadcast list of stokvel treasurers. Message them one at a time (never spam a group). Offer to set up their first stokvel in the app over a 15-min video call.
- **Amplifier:** get one respected treasurer (a "mama Sophie" figure) to record a 60-second video in isiZulu/Sesotho endorsing the app. Post everywhere.

### Loop 2 — The WhatsApp / TikTok Testimonial Loop 📱

- Kasi audiences trust people **who look like them**, not agencies.
- Pay 5 early users **R100 each** to record a 30-second phone video: *"I use KasiKash to track my [business]. It helps me because [reason]."* — in their home language.
- Post to **TikTok, Facebook Reels, Instagram Reels, and WhatsApp Status**. Same clip, 4 platforms.
- TikTok SA (~26M users) rewards kasi content that would flop with Silicon Valley polish.

### Loop 3 — The Google Loop 🔍 *(you already started — PR #30)*

- Structured data + sitemap done ✅
- **Next:** publish 3 SEO-focused blog posts on `kasikash.com/blog/`:
  1. *"How to prove your spaza shop's income to a bank in 2026"*
  2. *"Stokvel treasurer's guide: how to track contributions transparently"*
  3. *"Skoroskoro (customer tabs): how to get paid without losing customers"*
- These rank on long-tail queries. Free, compounding traffic forever.

## 📢 Phase 3 — Amplification (months 4–6)

**Goal: press, credibility, and the story spreads without you.**

### Free / earned media

Pitch these journalists with a specific data-driven angle, not "we launched an app":

| Outlet | Contact hint | Angle |
|---|---|---|
| **TechCentral** | Duncan McLeod (@mcleodd) | *"Fintech built entirely on-device, no data leaves the phone."* |
| **Ventureburn** / **Disrupt Africa** | editors@ventureburn.com | *"Solo founder, no funding, 500 users in kasi."* |
| **Daily Maverick — Business Maverick** | tips@dailymaverick.co.za | *"Why 60% of SA's economy is invisible to lenders — and how to fix it."* |
| **eNCA / Newzroom Afrika** | newsdesk@enca.com | *"A township credit score."* Bring a spaza owner on camera. |
| **Ukhozi FM / Motsweding FM / Umhlobo Wenene** | Producer emails on SABC site | Vernacular radio — massive under-used reach in townships. |
| **Yoco Blog** | editor@yoco.com | Guest post as an integration partner. |

### Ecosystem partnerships

- **SEDA** (Small Enterprise Development Agency) — actively looks for tools like yours to give beneficiaries.
- **Sefa** (Small Enterprise Finance Agency) — could become both a partner AND a lender customer.
- **GEP** (Gauteng Enterprise Propeller) — Gauteng-focused, natural fit if your beachhead is Gauteng.
- **SAB Foundation Tholoana Fund** — grant for SMMEs, they love enabling tools.
- **DTIC's Township & Rural Entrepreneurship Programme (TREP)** — this IS your ICP written in policy.

### Paid (only after the loops are working)

- **Skip Google Ads** — expensive, low intent from kasi audiences.
- **Meta/Facebook Reels ads** geo-targeted to Gauteng townships, in isiZulu.
- Budget: **R500/day for 2 weeks = R7k.** Test 4 different creator videos. Kill the losers, scale the winner.
- Expected CAC: **R15–R40 per install** if the creative is native SA and vernacular. Higher if it looks like an ad.

## 🧭 The one thing to internalise

> **Distribution > Product. In SA kasi markets, "distribution" means someone your customer trusts, showing them the app in their language.**

Every tactic above is a variation of that principle:

- Flyers at the taxi rank = trusted context
- Stokvel loop = trusted person
- Vernacular TikTok = trusted language
- Yoco partnership = trusted brand endorsing you
- Radio = trusted local voice

You already have a great product. Now stop building for a bit and go put it in front of one specific human, one taxi rank, one stokvel — and watch what happens.
